# Responsible use

This project generates a recognizable person. Use the LoRA and source images only when you have the subject's authorization and the necessary rights to the training media.

- Do not present generated speech, conduct, endorsements, or beliefs as authentic.
- Label public-facing clips as AI-generated or synthetic.
- Keep each output's `request.json` and `result.json`; they record the checkpoint hash, seed, prompt, and generation command.
- Do not use the system for fraud, political persuasion through impersonation, harassment, or non-consensual intimate content.
- Review every clip for facial/hand defects and misleading content before publication.
- Add provenance metadata or a visible disclosure in the publishing workflow; transcoding may strip embedded metadata.

The generated clips are silent. Adding a cloned voice or lip-sync increases impersonation risk and should require a separate consent and disclosure review.
